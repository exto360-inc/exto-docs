# Handover

The end of the line. When an asset's commissioning is finished, Exto compiles
its evidence into a **handover package** — a downloadable archive proving the
work was done.

## When an asset becomes eligible

Two rules, and the workbench chooses which applies.

| Rule | An asset is ready when |
| --- | --- |
| **Full completion** | Its status is completed, it is at 100%, and it has **no open gates**. |
| **Priority stages** | Every named stage is complete. Open gates still block it. |

The priority-stage rule exists for handing over on partial completion — the
commissioning that matters is done, even if peripheral stages are not. Open
gates block under **both** rules; a gate is never bypassed by naming stages
around it.

## Package states

| State | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Queued. Generation has not started. |
| **In progress** | Being compiled. |
| **Ready** | Complete and downloadable. |
| **Failed** | Generation did not finish. |
| **Invalidated** | It was ready, but the workbench's rule has since changed. |

**Invalidated** is the state worth understanding. The package is kept and stays
visible as a historical record, but it **cannot be downloaded** — it was built
under a rule that no longer applies. A fresh package is queued once the asset
satisfies the updated rule.

## Versions

Packages are versioned per asset, starting at 1. Reopening a completed asset and
completing it again produces version 2 rather than overwriting version 1, so an
asset handed over twice has both records.

## Generating and downloading

From the **Handover** section tab:

- **Generate** queues a package for an eligible asset.
- **Download** fetches a ready one as an archive.
- **Regenerate** builds a fresh version — after evidence changed, or after a
  failure.
- **Sweep** picks up the backlog, queuing everything now eligible.

### Downloading several at once

Selecting many assets merges their packages into **one archive**, each asset's
contents in its own folder named after it.

If the selection mixes ready and not-ready assets, you are asked what you want
rather than being given a partial download silently:

1. **Download the ready ones now.**
2. **Generate the rest** and wait.
3. **Cancel** and adjust the selection.

### Child packages

An asset with children exposes its children's packages too, so a parent can be
handed over with everything beneath it in one archive.

## What is in a package

The package is compiled from the asset's evidence — its completed stages, its
checklist answers, and the documents attached to them, with a cover.

Which is the practical argument for the discipline in
[Documents](/cx/documents): **evidence attached to the wrong thing does not make
it into the pack.** A photo dropped on the asset when a checklist item asked
for it leaves a gap in the record.

## When generation fails

A failed package names its failure. The common causes are evidence that cannot
be read and an asset whose eligibility changed mid-generation. Regenerate once
the cause is fixed; the failed record stays for the audit trail.

## Permissions

Generating and downloading follow the project's grants on the workbench —
the same grants that decide who may work its stages. Handover has no separate
permission of its own.

Eligibility is not a permission: an asset that does not meet the rule cannot
have a package generated by anyone, whatever their access.
