# Getting started

You sign in with your work email, complete your profile the first time, and
choose where you want to work. The app then opens on the main screen with four
tabs.

## Sign in

1. Open the Exto app. The **Sign In** screen asks you to "Enter your work email
    to get started".
2. Enter your work email in the **Work email** field and tap **Continue**.

<Shot src="mobile/sign-in" alt="The Sign In screen. Type your work email and tap Continue."
  caption="The Sign In screen. Type your work email and tap Continue." />

3. The next screen, "Choose how you'd like to continue", shows your email with
    a green tick and the sign-in methods your organisation allows, for example
    **Login** (with a password), **Continue with Microsoft** or **Continue with
    Google**. Tap **Use a different email** if the email is wrong.

<Shot src="mobile/method" alt="Choosing a sign-in method. Your email is confirmed at the top; pick one of the buttons below it."
  caption="Choosing a sign-in method. Your email is confirmed at the top; pick one of the buttons below it." />

4. Complete the sign-in page that opens in your browser. For example, for
    **Login** enter your password and tap **Continue**. A **Reset Password**
    link is available on that page.
5. When sign-in succeeds you return to the app automatically.

The app version is shown at the bottom of the sign-in page.

## Complete your profile (first sign-in only)

If your profile is incomplete, the app shows **Complete Your Profile**
("Complete your details for Exto to get started").

1. Check or fill in the fields shown, for example **Company Name**, **Job
    Role** and **Department**.
2. Tap **Submit Profile**.

<Shot src="mobile/complete-profile" alt="Complete Your Profile. Review the details and tap Submit Profile at the bottom."
  caption="Complete Your Profile. Review the details and tap Submit Profile at the bottom." />

## Choose your customer, instance and tenant

Next, choose where you want to work. Each screen shows a list of cards; tap one
to select it (a tick appears), then tap **Next**.

1. **Select Customer**: choose your organisation.
2. **Select Instance**: choose the environment. Each card shows the instance
    name, its web address and the number of tenants.
3. **Select Tenant**: choose the tenant you want to work in. Each card shows
    the tenant name and its Tenant ID. You can search the list.

If you have only one customer, instance or tenant, the app selects it for you
and skips that screen.

<Shot src="mobile/instance" alt="Select Instance. The selected card is highlighted and ticked. The badge on each card shows how many tenants it has."
  caption="Select Instance. The selected card is highlighted and ticked. The badge on each card shows how many tenants it has." />

<Shot src="mobile/select-tenant" alt="Select Tenant. Select a tenant and tap Next to open the app."
  caption="Select Tenant. Select a tenant and tap Next to open the app." />

::: tip Note

The tenant count on an instance card (for example "18 tenants") can be higher
than the number of tenants you see on the next screen.

:::

You can change tenant later from **Profile** (see [Change
tenant](/mobile/profile-and-settings#change-tenant)).

## The main screen

After sign-in the app opens on the main screen. Four tabs run along the bottom:

| Tab | Icon | What it is for |
|----|----|----|
| **Exto AI** | House | Ask questions about your data in plain language. |
| **Tasks** | List | Records that are waiting for you. |
| **Menu** | Grid of dots | Modules, dashboards, pages, Drive and other features you have access to. |
| **Profile** | Person | Your details, preferences, language, offline storage, feedback and log out. |

The selected tab is highlighted. When the app is offline, some features are
hidden or disabled and a banner at the top of the screen tells you so (see
[Working offline](/mobile/working-offline)).
