# Mobile app

**For people working from a phone** — in the field, on site, or away from a
desk.

Exto 3.0 is the mobile app for the Exto operations platform. It lets field and
project teams work on records, tasks, checklists and commissioning stages from a
phone, including when there is no internet connection.

New to the app? Start with the [Introduction](/mobile/introduction), then
[Getting started](/mobile/getting-started).

## Two things worth knowing first

::: tip Your Menu may look different
What each person sees in the Menu — modules, dashboards, pages — is configured
on the Exto web platform, so your Menu may not match the screenshots in this
manual.
:::

::: tip Download before you go offline
You can keep working without a connection, but only with the modules, tasks and
stages you downloaded first. See [Working offline](/mobile/working-offline).
:::
