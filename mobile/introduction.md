# Introduction

Exto 3.0 is the mobile app for the Exto operations platform. It lets field and
project teams work on records, tasks, checklists and commissioning stages from a
phone, including when there is no internet connection.

## Who it is for

- **Users**: people who fill in forms, act on tasks assigned to them, and upload
  documents.
- **PME users**: people responsible for monitoring and reporting on project
  execution. PME users can do everything a User can, and can also build
  checklist templates.

What each person sees in the Menu (modules, dashboards, pages) is configurable
on the Exto web platform, so your Menu may look different from the screenshots
in this manual.

## What you can do with the app

- Ask **Exto AI** questions about your project data.
- Work through the **Tasks** assigned to you.
- Create and update **records** in your modules and move them through their
  workflow.
- Maintain **master** (reference) data.
- View **dashboards** and **custom pages**.
- Store and share files in **Drive**.
- Build **checklist** templates (PME users).
- Track assets and stages in **Commissioning**.
- Download modules, tasks and stages and keep working **offline**.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|----|----|
| Customer | Your organisation's account on the Exto platform. |
| Instance | A copy of the platform (for example a live or test environment) with its own web address. |
| Tenant | A company or unit inside an instance. You work in one tenant at a time. |
| Context | Where a record belongs: the tenant, a workspace inside it, or a project inside a workspace. |
| Project | The level most work is done at, for example a building or site. |
| Module | A type of record configured on the web, for example "Issues At Site". |
| Record | One entry in a module, for example "Issues-8". |
| Workflow step | The stage a record is at. Each step offers actions such as Submit, Approve or Return. |
