# Profile and settings

The **Profile** tab ("Personalize your profile", the fourth tab) holds your
account details and app settings. Scroll down to see all sections, in this
order: Profile information, Customer (tenant), Display preferences, App
settings, Offline storage, More, Account management, About app and Log out.

## View or change your profile picture

1. Tap your picture at the top right. It opens full size with **Close** and
    **Edit**.
2. Tap **Edit** and choose a photo from your phone.
3. On **Edit Profile Picture**, drag and zoom the photo inside the square, use
    the rotate button or the **Scale** slider if needed, then tap ✓ (or ✕ to
    cancel).
4. The message "Profile picture updated" appears and your new picture is shown.

<Shot src="mobile/cropper" alt="Edit Profile Picture. Adjust the photo inside the grid and tap the tick at the top right."
  caption="Edit Profile Picture. Adjust the photo inside the grid and tap the tick at the top right." />

Changing the picture needs an internet connection.

## Profile information

Under **PROFILE INFORMATION**, your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email** and
**Role** (for example PME) are shown with a **Read Only** label. You cannot
change them in the app; the note "Your profile information is managed in
Console." tells you where to do it (see [Account management, app information and
log
out](/mobile/profile-and-settings#account-management-app-information-and-log-out)).

## Change tenant

The section labelled **TENANT** shows the tenant you are working in; it is the
tenant switcher.

1. Tap the card under **TENANT** (it shows the current tenant and "Manage
    Tenant").
2. The **Select Tenant** sheet lists your tenants. The current one is
    highlighted and ticked. Use **Search…** to find one.
3. Tap the tenant you want to switch to.

This needs an internet connection.

## Display preferences

Display preferences control how dates, times and amounts appear in the app (for
example chat times and record dates).

1. Under **DISPLAY PREFERENCES**, tap **Edit**.
2. In **Edit Display Preferences**, choose a **Date format** (for example
    DD/MM/YY or MM/DD), a **Time format** (for example HH:mm:ss) and a
    **Currency format** (for example Symbol).
3. Tap **Save** (or **Cancel**).
4. The message "Display preferences saved" appears and the new values are shown
    in the list.

  caption="Edit Display Preferences, with a dropdown for each format." />

## Language

1. Under **APP SETTINGS**, tap **Select Language**. The current language is
    shown on the right.
2. In the **Select Language** sheet, tap **English**, **Hindi**, **Tamil** or
    **French**. Use **Search Language...** to find one.
3. Tap **Apply**.

<Shot src="mobile/language" alt="The Select Language sheet. English is currently selected."
  caption="The Select Language sheet. English is currently selected." />

The app switches language straight away, without restarting.

<Shot src="mobile/french" alt="Profile after switching to French. Most labels are translated; some, such as OFFLINE STORAGE, still appear in English."
  caption="Profile after switching to French. Most labels are translated; some, such as OFFLINE STORAGE, still appear in English." />

Some texts are not yet translated and stay in English. Changing language needs
an internet connection.

## Offline storage

**OFFLINE STORAGE** shows what is stored on your phone for offline use. With
nothing downloaded it shows "No offline modules yet". Otherwise it shows:

- the total size, and the number of modules and records;
- **MODULES OFFLINE**, **TASKS OFFLINE** and stages offline, with the size of
  each item;
- **Sync now**: sends pending offline changes ("Nothing to sync" when there are
  none);
- **Refresh all snapshots**: updates the downloaded data;
- **Clear all offline data**.

Tap an item to open it.

<Shot src="mobile/offline-storage" alt="Offline storage with one module and one task downloaded, plus Sync now, Refresh all snapshots and Clear all offline data."
  caption="Offline storage with one module and one task downloaded, plus Sync now, Refresh all snapshots and Clear all offline data." />

### Clear all offline data

1. Tap **Clear all offline data**.
2. Read the warning: "All cached modules and offline records for this account
    will be deleted. This cannot be undone."
3. Type **CLEAR** in the box. **Clear all** becomes available only when the
    word matches.
4. Tap **Clear all**. The message "Offline data cleared" appears and the
    section shows "No offline modules yet".

<Shot src="mobile/clear" alt="The Clear all offline data? confirmation, with CLEAR typed in the box."
  caption="The Clear all offline data? confirmation, with CLEAR typed in the box." />

Sync any pending changes before clearing; the app may refuse to clear while
unsynced changes exist.

## Report an issue and Feedback

Use **Report an issue** to send the product team a screenshot and description of
a problem.

1. Under **MORE**, turn on **Report an issue**. A floating bug button appears
    on screen; drag it anywhere.

<Shot src="mobile/report-toggle" alt="Report an issue turned on. The floating bug button appears at the left of the screen."
  caption="Report an issue turned on. The floating bug button appears at the left of the screen." />

2. Go to the screen with the problem and tap the bug button. **Report a
    feedback** opens with a screenshot of that screen.
3. To mark the problem, tap the screenshot ("Tap to mark the issue"). In
    **Annotate Screenshot**, choose a colour and line width, draw on the image,
    then tap **Done**.
4. Under **Describe the issue**, type what went wrong ("What went wrong?").
5. Tap **Submit Report**. It is available once you have typed a description.

Tap **Feedback** under **MORE** to see the list of submitted reports. Reporting
needs an internet connection.

## Account management, app information and log out

- **Manage your account in Console**: tap **Open Console** to open the Exto
  Console in your browser, where you can update your personal details, password
  and account settings. When you return to the app, your profile is refreshed.
- **ABOUT APP**: shows the **App version** (for example 26.8.0) and **Build
  number**. Quote these when reporting a problem.
- **Log out**: tap the red button and confirm. Downloaded offline data stays on
  the phone.
