# Exto AI

Exto AI answers questions about your project data in plain language, for example
"which projects are nearing completion" or "show me a pie chart of open and
closed records". It can reply with text, charts and tables, ask you to clarify,
and read files you attach. Use it when you want an answer or summary without
searching through modules yourself.

Open it from the **Exto AI** tab (the first tab). Exto AI needs an internet
connection.

## Ask a question

1. Tap **Ask Exto AI** and type your question, or tap the microphone to speak
    it.
2. Tap the send button.
3. While Exto AI works, your message appears on the right and a card shows **AI
    is thinking...** with the steps it is taking. To cancel, tap the stop button
    that replaces the send button.

<Shot src="mobile/thinking" alt="Exto AI working on a question. The thinking card lists what it is doing."
  caption="Exto AI working on a question. The thinking card lists what it is doing." />

4. Read the reply. Replies can contain formatted text, lists, charts and
    tables. A summary card above the reply (for example "Replying to your
    greeting…") can be expanded to see how the answer was worked out.

<Shot src="mobile/chart" alt="A reply with a chart. Below each reply are buttons to give feedback, retry, copy the answer and have it read aloud."
  caption="A reply with a chart. Below each reply are buttons to give feedback, retry, copy the answer and have it read aloud." />

5. Sometimes Exto AI needs more information before it can answer. It then shows
    an **EXTO AI ASKS** card with a question and options. Choose an option (or
    type your answer) to continue. Your answer is recorded in the conversation.

<Shot src="mobile/asks" alt="An EXTO AI ASKS card asking which module scope to use. Tap one of the options to continue."
  caption="An EXTO AI ASKS card asking which module scope to use. Tap one of the options to continue." />

## Attach files

Attach files when you want Exto AI to answer questions about a document or
image.

1. Tap the paperclip in the message box.
2. Choose **Choose from Drive** to pick files already stored in Drive, or
    **Direct Upload** to pick files from your phone.

<Shot src="mobile/attach" alt="The attach sheet with its two options."
  caption="The attach sheet with its two options." />

3. If you chose Drive, open folders and tick the files you need ("Select from
    Drive (up to 3)"), then tap **Done**.

<Shot src="mobile/drive-picker" alt="Selecting files from Drive. Tap a folder to open it and tick the checkbox next to each file."
  caption="Selecting files from Drive. Tap a folder to open it and tick the checkbox next to each file." />

4. Type your question about the files and send it.

You can attach up to 3 files, each up to 10 MB.

## Manage conversations

- **New conversation**: tap **+** at the top right.
- **Chat History**: tap the clock icon. Conversations are grouped by date (for
  example Last Month, Older); pinned conversations appear at the top under
  **Pinned**. Use **Search** to find a conversation.
- **Reopen**: tap a conversation in the list.
- **Pin**: tap the pin icon next to a conversation. Tap it again to unpin.
- **Rename or delete**: long-press a conversation, then choose **Rename** (edit
  the name and tap **Save**) or **Delete** (confirm with **Delete** in the
  "Delete Chat" message).

## When you are offline

When there is no connection, a banner shows "You're offline. Some actions are
unavailable." and the message box reads "You're offline — reconnect to chat".
You can read the screen but cannot send messages.

<Shot src="mobile/chat-offline" alt="Exto AI offline. The banner and the disabled message box show that chat is unavailable."
  caption="Exto AI offline. The banner and the disabled message box show that chat is unavailable." />

## Notes

- AI replies are generated from your data and may vary. Check important figures
  in the source records.
- Message times use your chosen time format (see [Display
  preferences](/mobile/profile-and-settings#display-preferences)).
