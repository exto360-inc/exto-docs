# Your profile

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone, for themselves. Nothing here affects anyone else.
:::

Your profile holds the settings that change how Exto looks **to you** — date and
number formats, your theme, and whether AI summaries appear on records.

## Opening it

Click your **avatar**, at the top right of the screen, then choose **Account**.
The User Profile panel slides in from the right.

<Shot src="account/profile" alt="The User Profile panel"
  caption="Your identity on the left, read-only; the settings you can change on the right." />

## What is on it

**Down the left**, read-only: your picture, name, username, email and role. These
come from your account rather than being edited here.

**On the right**, the settings that are yours to change.

### Display preferences

These apply **everywhere in Exto** — every grid, every record, every export —
and only for you. A colleague looking at the same record sees it in their own
format.

| Setting | Decides |
| --- | --- |
| **Date format** | How dates read, e.g. `MM/DD/YYYY` or `DD/MM/YYYY`. |
| **Time format** | Whether you see seconds and milliseconds, and 12- or 24-hour. |
| **Currency format** | Whether amounts show a symbol or a code. |
| **Numeric display** | Plain numbers, or grouped with separators. |

::: tip Worth setting on day one
A colleague reading `03/04` as the fourth of March while you read it as the
third of April is a real source of error, and it takes ten seconds to prevent.
:::

### AI

| Setting | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Insights** | Whether AI-generated summaries appear on modules and records. Off is a perfectly normal choice. |
| **AI Memory** | Whether the assistant remembers facts and conversation summaries about you between conversations. |

Turning **AI Memory** off means each conversation starts fresh — the assistant
keeps nothing from the last one.

If neither setting is there, AI is switched off for the whole organisation.
That is an administrator's decision, not yours. See [AI](/ai/).

### Theme

Your theme is **not** in this panel. It is chosen from the same user menu, one
item below **Account**, under **Theme**.

<Shot src="account/theme" alt="The theme submenu"
  caption="Twelve themes, four of them dark. A tick marks the one you are using." />

Exto has no light/dark switch — picking a dark theme is how you get a dark
interface. See [How do I change the theme?](/recipes/account/change-theme).

## Saving

**Save** applies your changes; **Cancel** discards them. Changes take effect
immediately and follow you to any browser you sign in from.

## What is not here

::: warning Password and account changes happen in Console
Your **profile details, password, users and tenant settings** are managed in
**Console**, not in Exto. The link at the bottom of the panel opens it.

This is why your name and email are read-only here: Exto shows them, Console
owns them.
:::

So:

| To change | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Date, time, currency, number formats | **Here** |
| AI insights and memory | **Here** |
| Your theme | The user menu → Theme |
| Your password | **Console** |
| Your name, email, picture | **Console** |
| Who else has access | **Console**, or [Administering](/admin/) |
