# Your profile

Your details, how dates and numbers are shown to you, and your AI preferences.
Open it from your avatar in the top bar.

  caption="Personal information, display preferences, and the AI insight settings." />

## Personal information

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **First name** | Editable. |
| **Last name** | Editable. |
| **Username** | Editable. |
| **Email** | Your sign-in identity. |
| **Role** | Set by an administrator, not by you. |

You can also upload a **profile picture**, which is what appears on your
comments, history entries and avatar chips.

## Display preferences

These change how Exto renders values **for you only**. Nobody else's screen
changes.

| Preference | Affects |
| --- | --- |
| **Date format** | Every date in the product. |
| **Time format** | 12- or 24-hour. |
| **Currency format** | How money is displayed. |

::: tip Timestamps follow your profile, not your browser
Exto formats dates from these preferences rather than from your browser's
locale, so the same account reads the same way on every machine.
:::

## AI preferences

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **AI insights** | On or off. AI-generated summaries on modules and records. |
| **Show insights** | Automatically on open, or only when opened manually. |
| **Display as** | An inline banner above the form, or a side panel. |

With **AI insights** off, no insight is requested at all. See
[Record insights](/ai/insights).

These are your preferences, but they sit under an organisation-wide switch:
if **AI chat access** is off under **Settings → AI personalization**, AI is
unavailable regardless of what you set here.

## Password

Changing your password is under account settings, and applies only to password
sign-in. An SSO account's password lives with your identity provider. See
[Signing in](/account/signing-in).
