# Introduction

Exto tracks the readiness of physical assets — equipment, panels, systems —
from the moment they are installed to the moment they are handed over to the
people who will operate them.

Every asset has to pass a sequence of checks before it can be signed off:
documentation reviewed, installation verified, power proved, function tested.
Exto holds that sequence, records who did what, refuses to let a step be skipped
before its prerequisites are met, and compiles the evidence into a handover pack
at the end.

## How the pieces fit

Four ideas, and everything else follows from them.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Assets** | The things being made ready, arranged in a hierarchy — a building contains systems, a system contains equipment. |
| **Stages** | The sequence of checks each asset passes through, grouped into levels. |
| **Checklists** | What "done" means for one stage on one asset, answered pass, fail or not-applicable. |
| **Handover** | The evidence, compiled per asset once its checks are complete. |

The [CX workbench](/cx/) puts the first three on one screen: stages across,
assets down, and one unit of work at every intersection.

Around that sit the parts that make it usable on a real project —
**[modules](/modules/)** and **forms** for the records people fill in, **workflows** for who approves
what, **masters** for shared reference data, and **projects** to keep one
contractor's work separate from another's.

## Who does what

| You | Start at |
| --- | --- |
| **Commissioning** an asset — working checks, recording results | [CX workbench](/cx/) |
| **Doing the work** — filling forms, approving, inspecting | [Using Exto](/work/) |
| **Building** — designing the modules, forms and workflows others use | [Building](/build/) |
| **Administering** — setting up projects and deciding who sees what | [Administering](/admin/) |

If you only fill forms and approve things, you need **Using Exto** and nothing
else. That is the intended experience, not a limitation.

## How to use this guide

**Got a specific task?** [Recipes](/recipes/) answers one question each — *how do
I complete a checklist*, *why won't this stage close*, *how do I generate a
handover package* — and says up front whether you are allowed to do it.

**Can't sign in?** [Signing in](/account/signing-in) covers password, one-time
code and single sign-on, and what each failure message means. Nothing else here
helps until you are in.

**Just been given an account?** [Quick start](/getting-started/quick-start)
covers what you are looking at and where to go first.

**About to build something?** [Core concepts](/concepts/) first. Modules,
records, workflow versions and permissions come up on every page that follows.

::: tip When something is missing or greyed out
It is almost always a permission, and four separate systems can be responsible.
[Permissions](/concepts/permissions) explains how to tell which one is in your
way, and [Diagnosing "I can't see it"](/recipes/cant-see-it) walks through it
step by step.
:::
