# Quick start

What to expect the first time you open Exto. This page is for people who have
just been given an account — it does not build anything.

<Shot src="getting-started/quick-start" alt="The Exto home screen"
  caption="Signing in lands you on your home page — usually My tasks or a dashboard." />

## 1. Signing in

You cannot sign yourself up. An administrator creates your account, and you
arrive by invitation or by being given a sign-in address.

Sign-in goes through **Exto ID**, shared across the Exto suite. Enter your email
on **Sign in to continue**, choose **Continue**, and authenticate however your
organisation is set up — a password, a one-time code, or your company's single
sign-on.

See [Signing in](/account/signing-in), which also covers what to do when it
fails.

## 2. Choosing a tenant

If you have access to more than one **tenant** — a separate, isolated copy of
Exto, often one for live work and one for training — you are asked to pick one
after signing in.

::: warning Data never crosses between tenants
A record in the training tenant does not exist in the live one. If something you
expect is missing, check which tenant you are in before anything else. See
[Choosing a tenant](/account/tenants).
:::

## 3. Where you land

You arrive at a **landing page** chosen by your administrator — often
[My tasks](/work/my-tasks), sometimes a dashboard. If none is set, you land on
Projects.

There is nothing to fix if it is not what you expected; it is a setting, and an
administrator can change it. See [Menu configuration](/menu/).

## 4. Reading the screen

Three parts, and they behave the same everywhere in the product.

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **The sidebar** | Your menu. It shows only what you have access to, so it is shorter for some people than others. |
| **The main area** | Usually a grid of records. Filter it, group it, and open a row to see one record. |
| **The right-hand rail** | On an open record: its actions, people, comments, files and history. See [Record widgets](/work/record-widgets). |

::: tip A short sidebar is not a fault
The menu is built from your permissions. If a colleague can see something you
cannot, that is a [group grant](/concepts/permissions), not a bug — and
[Diagnosing "I can't see it"](/recipes/cant-see-it) walks through finding out
which layer is responsible.
:::

## 5. Your first real task

Most people's day starts at **[My tasks](/work/my-tasks)** — every record
waiting on you, across every module, in one list.

Open one, fill in what it asks for, and press the action that moves it on —
**Submit**, **Approve**, **Return**. That is the whole loop, and
[Taking actions](/work/taking-actions) explains what each one does.

::: warning Saving is not submitting
**Save** keeps your changes and leaves the record exactly where it is. Nobody
is notified, and nothing moves. If a colleague cannot find something you
finished, this is almost always why.
:::

## 6. Making it yours

Two settings worth changing on day one, both in
[your profile](/account/profile):

- **Date and number format** — so dates read the way you expect.
- **AI insights** — whether an AI summary appears on records, and where.
