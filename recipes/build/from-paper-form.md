# How do I turn a paper form into a digital one?

::: tip Who can do this
<Perm role="PME" /> — the application role that opens **Settings**.
:::

The **module builder** reads a photograph or PDF of a paper form and builds the
module, the form, the checklist and a starter workflow from it. It is the fast
path; the [module designer](/build/module-designer) is the complete one.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Module builder**.
2. Choose **Create new module**, and upload the document — PNG, JPEG, WebP or
   PDF.
3. Review what came back. Give the module a display name and pick its kind.
4. **Form** — name it and review the proposed fields.
5. **Checklist** — appears only if the form needs one. You can skip it.
6. **Preview** — drag fields to get the order right. Much quicker here than
   later.
7. **Workflow** — create a one-step workflow.

## Result

A working module, and a **first draft** rather than a finished design.

::: warning It reads layout, not intent
The wizard is good at getting eighteen fields off a scanned page. It has no
opinion about which your business needs, which must be required, or who
approves them.
:::

## Next

Open it in the module designer and: check the General tab, fix field types the
read got wrong, add [rules](/build/rules), and replace the one-step workflow
with a real one.
