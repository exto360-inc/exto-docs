# How do I turn AI on for my organisation?

::: tip Who can do this
<Perm role="PME" /> — the application role that opens **Settings**.
:::

One switch governs every AI feature — the assistant, record insights and the AI
form builder.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → AI personalization**.
2. Switch AI on for the organisation.
3. Save.

## Result

AI features become available. Individuals can still turn **insights** off for
themselves in [their profile](/account/profile); the organisation switch is the
outer gate.

::: warning Turning it on is not the same as making it useful
The assistant answers from what somebody has told it your fields mean. With the
switch on and nothing curated, it answers in column names.
:::
