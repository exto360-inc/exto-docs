# How do I add someone to a project?

::: tip Who can do this
`Project setting` + `Users`, in the same context. Inviting needs `Invite users`.
:::

Assignment puts somebody **in** the project. It grants them nothing on its own —
access comes from the groups they are then added to.

## Steps

1. Open the project and go to its **Users** tab.
2. Choose **Assign users**, and pick the people.
3. If they have no account yet, **invite** them instead.
4. Go to the **Groups** tab and add them to the groups that carry their access.

## Result

They can reach the project, and see whatever their groups grant.

::: tip Two steps, not one
Assigning without adding to a group produces somebody who is in the project and
can see nothing. That is the most common cause of "I've been added but there's
nothing there".
:::
