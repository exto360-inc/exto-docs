# How do I complete a checklist on a cell?

::: tip Who can do this
**Edit** on that stage, granted to your project groups.
:::

A cell is one stage on one asset — the unit of commissioning work. Where the
stage has a checklist, that is what you work through.

## Steps

1. Open the [matrix](/cx/matrix) and click the cell.
2. If no record exists yet you are prompted to create one — it arrives already
   stamped with its asset and stage.
3. Work down the checklist, answering each item **Pass**, **Fail** or **N/A**.
4. Add notes, attachments and linked records where the item asks for them.
5. Set the cell's **status**.
6. Save.

## Result

The cell's colour changes and its progress rolls up — to the asset, and from
there up the asset hierarchy.

::: tip The checklist decides the percentage
A stage **with** a checklist takes its percentage from the pass and fail counts.
A stage **without** one is binary: 0% or 100% by status. That is why attaching a
checklist changes how progress reads, not just what the cell shows.
:::

::: warning Attach evidence to the item, not the asset
If a checklist item asks for an attachment, put it there. Dropped on the asset
instead, the item stays incomplete and the file will not reach the
[handover pack](./generate-handover).
:::
