# How do I generate a handover package?

::: tip Who can do this
The project's grants on the workbench.
:::

When an asset's commissioning is finished, its evidence compiles into a
downloadable archive proving the work was done.

## Steps

1. Open the workbench's **Handover** section tab.
2. Find the asset. Eligible assets can be generated; others say why not.
3. Choose **Generate**.
4. Wait — packages are queued, not built on the spot.
5. When it reaches **Ready**, choose **Download**.

## Result

An archive containing the asset's completed stages, checklist answers and
attached documents.

An asset becomes eligible under one of two rules, depending on the workbench:

| Rule | Eligible when |
| --- | --- |
| **Full completion** | Completed, at 100%, with **no open gates**. |
| **Priority stages** | Every named stage is complete. Open gates still block. |

::: warning Open gates block under both rules
A gate is never bypassed by naming stages around it. See
[How do I find out why a stage won't close?](./stage-wont-close).
:::

## If a package says "invalidated"

It was ready, but the workbench's eligibility rule changed afterwards. It stays
visible as a historical record and cannot be downloaded; a fresh package is
queued once the asset satisfies the new rule.
