# How do I download packages for several assets at once?

::: tip Who can do this
The project's grants on the workbench.
:::

Selecting many assets merges their packages into **one archive**, each asset's
contents in its own folder named after it.

## Steps

1. Open the **Handover** section tab.
2. Select the assets you want.
3. Choose **Download**.
4. If the selection mixes ready and not-ready assets, choose what to do.

## Result

One archive, foldered by asset.

When the selection is mixed you are asked rather than given a partial download
silently:

1. **Download the ready ones now.**
2. **Generate the rest** and wait.
3. **Cancel** and adjust the selection.

::: tip A parent brings its children
An asset with children exposes their packages too, so a system can be handed
over with everything beneath it in one archive.
:::
