# How do I change who a record is assigned to?

::: tip Who can do this
The current step's assignees, where the action allows assignees to be changed.
:::

By default a record goes to whoever is configured for the next step. Some
actions let you choose instead — useful when a step has ten possible approvers
and only one of them should see it.

## Steps

1. Open the record and open the **Actions** widget.
2. If the action permits it, a **Change assignees** control appears beside it.
3. Pick the people or groups the record should go to.
4. Take the action.

## Result

The record moves to the next step assigned to the people you chose, and only
they see it in their tasks.

If the control is not there, that action was configured not to allow it — a
designer sets this per action in [Steps & actions](/build/steps-and-actions).

::: tip Adding someone to a record that needs them
A module owner can also add a **responsible person** to a record on a later
step, from the **People** widget, without moving the record at all.
:::
