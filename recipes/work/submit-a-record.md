# How do I submit a record for approval?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone who can create or edit the record.
:::

Filling a form in and saving it does **not** send it anywhere. Submitting is a
separate, deliberate step that moves the record to the next person.

## Steps

1. Open the record and fill in the form.
2. Open the **Actions** widget on the right-hand rail.
3. Press **Submit**.
4. Add a comment if one is asked for — many actions require one.

## Result

The record moves to the next workflow step, takes a new status, and the people
responsible for that step are notified. It appears in their
[My tasks](/work/my-tasks) and leaves your drafts.

::: warning Save is not Submit
**Save** keeps your changes and leaves the record exactly where it is. Nobody is
notified and nothing moves. If a colleague cannot find something you finished,
this is almost always why.
:::
