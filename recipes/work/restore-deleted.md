# How do I restore something I deleted by mistake?

::: tip Who can do this
You see the items you had access to delete, in the scopes you can reach.
:::

Deleted records, documents and folders go to the **recycle bin** rather than
disappearing.

## Steps

1. Open **Recycle bin** from the sidebar.
2. Find the item — filter by type, module or who deleted it.
3. Choose **Restore**.

## Result

The item returns to where it was, with its data intact.

::: warning Retention is limited
Items are permanently deleted when their retention expires. If something matters
and you are still deciding, **flag it for review** — that extends its retention
by seven days and holds it.

Flagging locks for seven days: you cannot unflag it during the review period.
:::

## If it is not there

The recycle bin holds things that were **deleted**. If a record still exists but
you cannot see it, that is an access question, not a deletion — see
[How do I find out why someone can't see something?](../admin/diagnose-access).
