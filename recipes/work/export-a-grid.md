# How do I export what I'm looking at?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone with view access to the grid.
:::

Every grid exports — record lists, master data, My tasks, job status and the CX
views.

## Steps

1. Set the grid up the way you want it exported: filters, visible columns, sort.
2. Choose **Export**.

## Result

A spreadsheet of the **active view** — its filters, its visible columns, in
their current order and sort.

::: warning Export follows the view, not the module
A filtered grid exports the filtered set. Clear your filters first if you want
everything, and check which columns are hidden before concluding a field is
missing from the data.
:::

For a formatted document rather than raw rows, use a [report](/work/reports)
instead.
