# How do I approve or reject a record?

::: tip Who can do this
The current step's assignees — directly or through a group.
:::

If a record is waiting on you it appears in [My tasks](/work/my-tasks). The
buttons you get are whatever the workflow draws leaving your step.

## Steps

1. Open the record from **My tasks**.
2. Read it, and check its [history](/work/history) if you need to see what
   changed.
3. Open the **Actions** widget.
4. Press **Approve** or **Reject**.
5. Enter a comment. Most actions require one.

## Result

The record moves on and takes the status that action stamps. Your comment is
attached **to that decision**, not just to the record — so the reasoning stays
with the action rather than drifting down a thread.

How many people must act depends on the step's completion rule:

| Rule | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **One** | Your action moves it, and it leaves everyone else's list. |
| **All** | Every assignee must act before it moves. |
| **Majority** | More than half must act. |
