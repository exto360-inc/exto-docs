# How do I save a view I use every day?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone can save a personal view. Sharing one depends on the grant on that grid.
:::

A **view** remembers a filter, which columns are shown, their order, and the
sort. Rebuilding the same filter every morning is the thing views exist to stop.

## Steps

1. Open the grid and set it up the way you want — filter, sort, show and hide
   columns.
2. Click **View** at the right of the filter bar to open its settings.
3. Set **Fields**, **Group by** and **Slice by**, and **Name** it for the
   **question it answers** — *Awaiting my approval* — not for how it is built.
4. Choose **Save view**.

For a new one rather than a change to the current view, use **+ New view**
beside the view tabs.

## Result

The view appears as a chip above the grid. Selecting it restores everything at
once. Views can be renamed, duplicated, reordered and hidden.

::: tip Shared views are a small act of design
Everyone arriving at that module sees what you decided mattered. Name them for
the reader, not for the mechanism.
:::

The same machinery runs on [My tasks](/work/my-tasks), master grids, job status
and the [CX workbench](/cx/matrix), so this works everywhere.
