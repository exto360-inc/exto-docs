# How do I comment on a record, or mention someone?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone who can open the record.
:::

Comments live on the record rather than in email, so the discussion stays with
the thing it is about.

## Steps

1. Open the record.
2. Open the **Comments** widget on the right-hand rail.
3. Type your comment. Formatting is available.
4. To pull somebody in, type **@** and start typing their name, then pick them.
5. Post it.

## Result

The comment appears on the record for anyone who can see it. Mentioning somebody
notifies them, and their name becomes a hover card.

::: tip A comment on an action stays with that decision
When you comment as part of **Submit**, **Approve** or **Return**, it is
attributed to that action rather than sitting loose in the thread. Six months
later, the reason and the decision are still together.
:::
