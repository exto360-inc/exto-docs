# How do I find the records waiting for me?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone. There is nothing to grant.
:::

**My tasks** lists every record sitting on a workflow step you are responsible
for — across every module, in one grid. It is the page most people start their
day on.

## Steps

1. Click the **My Tasks** icon in the header, at the top right.
   It slides in over whatever you are looking at.
2. Use **Slice by** in the left panel to narrow by module, status or step.
3. Click a row to open the record.

## Result

You see only records where you are a current step assignee — directly, or
through a group you belong to. Nothing else appears, which is why My tasks is
usually much shorter than a module's full record list.

A record leaves this list the moment it moves on. If the step's completion rule
is **one**, somebody else acting on it removes it from your list too.

::: warning Can't find something you know exists?
It may not be on your step. Use the module's
[record list](/work/record-list) instead — that shows every record you have
access to, not just the ones waiting on you.
:::
