# How do I turn off AI insights on records?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone, for themselves.
:::

## Steps

1. Click your **avatar** at the top right, then **Account**.
2. Under **AI**, switch **AI Insights** off.
3. **Save**.

**AI Memory** sits just below it — that one controls whether the assistant
remembers things about you between conversations, and is separate.

## Result

Insights behave the way you chose, for you only.

::: tip If the setting is not there
AI is off for the whole organisation. That is an administrator's switch, not
yours — see [How do I turn AI on for my organisation?](../integrations/turn-on-ai).
:::
