# How do I change the theme?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone, for themselves.
:::

Exto has **no light/dark toggle**. It has twelve named themes, four of which are
dark — choosing a dark theme is how you get a dark interface.

<Shot src="account/theme" alt="The theme submenu"
  caption="Twelve themes, each with its colour. A tick marks the one you are using." />

## Steps

1. Open the **user menu** — your avatar, top right.
2. Choose **Theme**.
3. Pick one. Each shows a colour dot, and a tick marks your current choice.

## Result

The interface changes immediately. **Graphite Cobalt** is the default.

| | Themes |
| --- | --- |
| **Light** | Exto Indigo, Graphite Cobalt, Organic Nature, Carbon Emerald, Slate Amber, Porcelain Teal, Arctic Minimal, Midnight Indigo |
| **Dark** | Electric Slate, Midnight Cyan, Nebula AI, Cyber Slate |

::: tip It is yours alone
The theme is stored in your own browser, per person — not per tenant. Nobody
else is affected, and it does not follow you to another machine.
:::

The **sign-in page** is always light, whatever you pick.
