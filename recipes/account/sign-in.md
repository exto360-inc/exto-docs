# How do I sign in, and what if I can't?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone with an account. You cannot sign yourself up.
:::

Exto signs you in through **Exto ID**, a single sign-in shared across the Exto
suite. You give it your email and it works out the rest.

## Steps

1. Open your organisation's Exto address.
2. On **Sign in to continue**, enter your **email address**.
3. Choose **Continue**.
4. Authenticate the way your organisation is set up — password, a one-time code,
   or your company's single sign-on.

## Result

You are returned to Exto and land on your home page — often
[My tasks](/work/my-tasks). If you can reach more than one tenant, you pick one
first.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Stuck on **Redirecting to secure sign-in…** | Exto ID could not be reached. Reload; if it persists, tell your administrator. |
| Email not recognised | Your account may be under a different address. Check the one your invitation came to. |
| You authenticate, but Exto refuses | Your Exto ID account has no access to this instance. An administrator grants it. |
| A maintenance page | The instance is deliberately down. Wait. |
| Signed in, but nothing looks familiar | Wrong [tenant](./switch-tenant) — that is far more common than it sounds. |

::: warning Leaving Exto does not sign you out of Exto ID
The sign-in is shared across products. On a shared machine, sign out of Exto ID
itself.
:::
