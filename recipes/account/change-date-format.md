# How do I change my date and number format?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone, for themselves.
:::

Formats are **yours**, not the tenant's. Changing them affects only what you
see.

## Steps

1. Click your **avatar** at the top right, then **Account**.
2. Under **Display Preferences**, set your **date format**, **time format**,
   **currency format** and **numeric display**.
3. **Save**.

## Result

Dates and numbers render your way everywhere — grids, records, reference tables
and exports.

::: tip Worth doing on day one
A colleague reading `03/04` as the fourth of March while you read it as the
third of April is a real source of error, and it takes ten seconds to prevent.
:::
