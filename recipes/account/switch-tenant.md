# How do I switch to a different tenant?

::: tip Who can do this
Anyone with access to more than one tenant.
:::

A **tenant** is a separate, isolated copy of Exto — often one for live work and
one for training.

## Steps

1. Click the **tenant button at the top of the sidebar** — it shows your current
   tenant and its plan.
2. **Search** by name if the list is long.
3. Click the tenant you want. The current one is marked **Current**.

## Result

You are now working in that tenant. Everything you see belongs to it.

::: warning Data never crosses between tenants
A record in the training tenant does not exist in the live one. If something you
expect is missing, check which tenant you are in **before** anything else — this
is the single most common cause of "the record has disappeared".
:::
