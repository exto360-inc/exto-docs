# Level handover checklist

A level is ready for handover when every item below is closed.

1. All fire dampers installed, wired and witness-tested.
2. Emergency lighting passes the full 90-minute duration test.
3. Cable trays labelled on every riser.
4. Pipework pressure tests passed and certificates filed.
5. As-built drawings issued for every riser.
6. Snag list signed off by the client representative.
