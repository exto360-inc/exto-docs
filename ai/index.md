# AI

Three AI features, doing three different jobs.

| Feature | Does |
| --- | --- |
| [Assistant](/ai/assistant) | Answers questions about your data, in chat. |
| [Record insights](/ai/insights) | Summarises one record, on the record. |
| [AI form builder](/ai/form-builder) | Reads a document and drafts a form from it. |

A fourth, the [module builder](/build/module-builder), reads a document and
builds a whole module — form, checklist and workflow — rather than one form.

## What they share

All three are grounded in your tenant's own data and schema. How well they
work depends heavily on two things an administrator controls:

- **Module descriptions** — written in business terms, in the
  [module designer](/build/module-designer).
- **The schema editor** — display names, descriptions and synonyms per field.
  See [Data setup](/admin/data-setup), which also covers the knowledge graph
  and the sync that makes curation take effect.

Curating those is the difference between an assistant that answers in your
vocabulary and one that answers in column names.

## Turning AI on and off

| Control | Scope | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI chat access** | The whole organisation | Settings → AI personalization |
| **Custom instructions** and **terminology** | The whole organisation | Settings → AI personalization |
| **AI insights** | One user | [Your profile](/account/profile) |
| **Insight placement and trigger** | One user | [Your profile](/account/profile) |

The organisation-wide switch wins. With AI chat access off, the assistant is
unavailable to everyone regardless of individual preferences.

## What AI does not do

- It does not take workflow actions. Moving a record is always a person; see
  [Taking actions](/work/taking-actions).
- It does not widen access. The assistant answers from data you can already
  see — it is not a way around [permissions](/concepts/permissions).
- It does not edit records.
